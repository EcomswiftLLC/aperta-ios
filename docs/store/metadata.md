Description (en-US). Honest wording: no star or review claims, no "original" for scaled copies, no promise that blur hides anything, and the ad model stated plainly. Paste into App Store Connect as is.

```
Edit, combine and resize photos on your iPhone or iPad. Aperta is a free photo studio that keeps your pictures on your device. There is no account, and nothing is uploaded.

EDIT
- Crop, straighten, rotate and flip
- 13 adjustment dials and auto enhance
- 24 filters: 17 made for Aperta and 7 built on Apple's photo effects
- Text, stickers, emoji, shapes and drawing
- A blur and pixelate brush to soften a face, a number plate or an address. These effects reduce detail but cannot promise to hide something, so cover anything sensitive with a drawing stroke
- Remove the background, on your device
- Frames, borders and canvas shapes
- Save as JPG, PNG or HEIC

MORE TOOLS
- Collage: combine two to nine photos
- Compress: make files smaller, with the quality you choose
- Resize: exact sizes, and presets for popular social networks
- Convert: HEIC, JPG and PNG, or photos to one PDF
- Watermark: add your name or logo to many photos at once
- ID photo: passport and visa photo sizes with a print sheet. Rules differ by country and change over time, so check the requirements of the office that issues your document
- Photo details: see location and camera details, then save a copy without the location, place names and common identifying details such as serial numbers and owner names

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Editing, background removal and every other tool run on your device. Your photos are never uploaded. Very large photos are scaled down when they are opened (up to 4096 pixels on the long side in the batch tools, 3072 in Collage and 6144 in the editor), and the app tells you when that happens.

FREE, WITH ADS
Aperta is free. Ads from Google AdMob pay for it: a banner on the Home screen and, before you save or share a finished photo, one short video that you choose to watch. One watched ad covers your exports for 2 minutes. If no ad is available, your export goes ahead. If you allow tracking when iOS asks, the ads may use your advertising identifier. The app works the same either way.

Questions or ideas? Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.
```

Facts behind the wording (keep them true when the app changes): 24 filters = 17 made for Aperta + 7 Apple CIPhotoEffect looks (Instant, Chrome, Noir, Mono, Tonal, Fade, Transfer); size caps are PhotoLoader.batchMaxPixel, collageMaxPixel, editorMaxPixel; the export chip says "Full size" only when nothing was scaled, otherwise "Up to N px".
